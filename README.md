# CallRescue ⚡

> **White-Label Speed-to-Lead & Missed-Call SMS Auto-Responder for Agencies**

CallRescue is an enterprise-grade, turn-key white-label software platform designed for marketing agencies, local SEO consultants, and digital freelancers to resell to dental clinics, home service contractors (HVAC/plumbers), legal firms, and local businesses.

---

### Core Capabilities

1. **⚡ 4-Second Instant Missed-Call Intercept Engine**
   - Automatically intercepts unanswered and busy phone calls via telecom conditional call forwarding (`*71`).
   - Fires a natural, personalized 2-way SMS in under 4 seconds to capture leads before they call competitors on Google Maps.

2. **💬 Two-Way Speed-to-Lead SMS Hub**
   - Live receptionist inbox with multi-staff attribution.
   - Quick response snippet chips (booking links, office directions, emergency intake forms).
   - 1-Click Twilio voice dialer to call customers back immediately.

3. **⚙️ Speed-to-Lead Automation & Webchat-to-SMS**
   - Human-mimic typing delay selector (0s, 3s, 5s).
   - Business-hours vs. After-hours auto-responder templates.
   - Floating website chat-to-SMS lead capture bubble.
   - Critical keyword emergency alert escalation.

4. **📡 Carrier Forwarding & Visual No-Code Webhook Field Mapper**
   - Works with all major carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, VOIP) via simple `*71` dial codes.
   - No-code visual JSON schema parser for Twilio, Telnyx, and RingCentral payloads.
   - Real-time CRM lead sync (Dentrix, Jobber, ServiceTitan).

5. **💳 Client Retainer Billing & Self-Healing Dunning Bot**
   - Track monthly recurring retainers (\$149–\$299/mo) charged to clients via Stripe Connect.
   - Retainer Milestone Expansion trigger alert (e.g. 42 calls rescued $\to$ 1-click upsell to 24/7 AI Voice Bot).
   - Self-Healing 1-Click Retainer Dunning Bot with dynamic speed-to-lead value-grounding ($45,000 preserved revenue).

6. **🏷️ Agency White-Label & DNS Hub**
   - Custom CNAME masking (`call.youragency.com`) with automated SSL.
   - Client sub-user portal simulation (wholesale fees & agency margins 100% hidden).
   - Automated 1st-of-the-month executive deliverable PDF emailer.
   - White-label Progressive Web App (PWA) with custom homescreen icons.

7. **📱 Front-Desk PIN Reception Kiosk (`/kiosk`)**
   - 4-digit staff PIN lock screen (`1234`).
   - Rapid manual caller text-back puncher and real-time missed call recovery queue.

---

### Tech Stack
- **Framework:** Vue 3 (Composition API, `<script setup>`)
- **State Management:** Pinia (with `localStorage` persistence)
- **Routing:** Vue Router 4
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + Custom Glassmorphism UI
- **Icons:** Lucide Icons (`lucide-vue-next`)
- **Build Tool:** Vite 5
